Birds of New York City

Each March and April, millions of birds pass through New York City as part of their spring migration. Traveling from the Caribbean up to Nova Scotia or even Greenland, these birds stop to eat, sleep, and socialize in the city's parks, trees, and waterways. New York City is located on one of the four major migration corridors in North America, a winged corridor known as the "Atlantic Flyway." Spring bird-watching in New York City picks up in March and reaches its peak in April-May. During this time, hundreds of species will stop to visit the city.
